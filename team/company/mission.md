# AI OFFICE — COMPANY MISSION & PRODUCTION PROFILE

## 1. Corporate Mission
AI Office is an autonomous artificial intelligence media company engineered to operate 24/7 on commodity ARM64 hardware (Android 16 Termux / Ubuntu PRoot). The company produces high-engagement short-form and high-retention long-form audiovisual content across multiple digital distribution channels.

## 2. Core Business Strategy
The company does not blindly duplicate single video assets across channels. Instead, it engineers a **Master Content Project** for each approved topic and generates distinct platform-tailored variations.

### Output Profiles:

#### Short-Form Productions (Vertical 9:16)
- **Target Resolution**: 1080 × 1920 progressive
- **Target Platforms**: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels
- **Durations**:
  - TikTok: 15s to 60s
  - YouTube Shorts: 30s to 58s
  - Instagram Reels: 15s to 45s
- **Structure**: High-impact visual hook (0–3s), problem / conflict escalation (3–20s), rapid resolution / twist (20–45s), call to action.
- **Visuals & Subtitles**: Full-screen dynamic typography, high-contrast captions, rhythmic pacing, kinetic scene cuts.

#### Long-Form Productions (Horizontal 16:9)
- **Target Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 progressive (Full HD)
- **Target Platforms**: YouTube long-form, Facebook Video
- **Durations**: 5 minutes to 20 minutes
- **Structure**: Narrative documentary, deep-dive educational, historical chronicle, or technology forecast.
- **Structure**: Teaser hook, chaptered narrative arc, contextual b-roll, atmospheric score, dynamic subtitle integration, custom high-CTR thumbnail concept, chapter metadata.

## 3. Autonomous Topic Selection
AI Office selects niches and topics based on objective research:
1. Search intent and trend velocity
2. Competitive saturation and freshness
3. Production feasibility within authorized free tooling
4. Engagement and monetization potential
