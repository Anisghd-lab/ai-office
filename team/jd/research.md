# DEPARTMENT JOB DESCRIPTION: RESEARCH & INTELLIGENCE

**Department ID**: `research`  
**Director**: Dr. Aris Vance  
**Operational Status**: **ACTIVE**  
**Governing Contract**: Authoritative departmental charter  

---

## 1. Primary Mission
Discover, evaluate, score, and verify high-potential audiovisual content opportunities across global digital video platforms without ever fabricating trends, data, or source citations.

## 2. Core Responsibilities
1. **Trend Discovery & Velocity Analysis**: Scan search intent, breakout topics, algorithmic surges, and audience discussions.
2. **Niche & Competitor Mapping**: Identify content saturation levels, competitive weaknesses, and underserved sub-niches.
3. **Audience Research & Psychology**: Determine viewer demographics, emotional triggers, and core watch motivations.
4. **Source Verification & Fact Checking**: Collect empirical citations and credible primary sources for every factual assertion.
5. **Content Opportunity Scoring**: Quantify each proposal based on trend freshness, competition, production feasibility, and monetization score (0.0 to 10.0).

## 3. Required Deliverable Specification (Research Report)
Every research deliverable must adhere to the standard schema:
- **Topic Title & Identifier**: Unique project opportunity handle.
- **Target Audience Profile**: Demographics, interests, and viewer intent.
- **Evidence & Primary Sources**: Verifiable URLs, documentation, or historical records.
- **Trend Relevance & Freshness**: Current trajectory and search volume indicators.
- **Competitive Landscape**: Top performing videos and gaps in existing coverage.
- **Potential Content Formats**: Short-form (9:16 vertical) and/or long-form (16:9 narrative).
- **Production Risks & Feasibility**: Asset constraints, model limitations, or policy boundaries.
- **Strategic Recommendation**: Clear GO / NO-GO verdict with confidence rating (0–100%).

## 4. Operational Invariant
- **Strict Anti-Hallucination**: Never invent statistics, audience metrics, or fake source links. If data cannot be determined, mark it `UNKNOWN`.
